## Rules

`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/lite/docs/SHARED-RULES.md` and `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/lite/playbooks/hybrid-rules.md` apply. `disallowedTools: Agent`. Spends nothing: backgrounds and motion transfer are generated by the orchestrator after a yes. Run from the workspace root. `S` is `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/lite/scripts`. Never redraw a person: they reach the master as the shot's own pixels.

## Cut-out (hybrid stage, first pass)

`python S/cutout.py {client} {job-id}` (or `--panel S03`). Look at each `previews/hybrid/{id}-mask.png`. Coverage under 2% or over 85%, a missing hand or product, or a hole in the person: retry that shot (`--lo 0.12..0.3` to keep more edge, `--hi 0.5..0.75` for a harder matte, on a screen; a grey (part see-through) body in the mask means `--hi` lower; `--method rembg` without one; `--model birefnet-general-lite` for fine hair, slow). Report each shot's method and coverage.

## Composite (hybrid stage, after backgrounds land)

`python S/composite.py {client} {job-id}`. Every shot reports `actor kept` (97% or more passes). A flagged shot: look at its before/after, say what is wrong (edge halo, brightness mismatch: `--match 0.3..0.8`), retry once, then report it flagged. Never call a flagged shot passed.

## Grade

`python S/grade.py {client} {job-id} --stills` first; look at `previews/grade/*.jpg` for skin, crushed blacks and clipped highlights. Too strong: `--strength 0.6`. Then `python S/grade.py {client} {job-id}` for every shot.

## Motion graphics

`node S/mograph.js {client} {job-id} plan`. None: report "skip". Otherwise `node S/mograph-setup.js --check` (not ready: say so; the orchestrator asks the person to run the one-time setup), `mograph.js run --sample`, look at the PNG it writes, then `mograph.js run`. Props per `playbooks/mograph-rules.md`.

## Render

`python S/render.py {client} {job-id}` (`--prores` when the brief asks for a mezzanine). It must print `checks: ok`; a failed check is the result, reported as is. Report the master's path, frame count and duration.
